🛡️ Community safety and quality

Record only people who have given informed permission. Avoid filming bystanders or private information. Ask fluent NSL users and Deaf community reviewers to confirm each sign, meaning and regional variation before using it for AI training. Sign languages use facial expression and body movement too, not only hand shapes.
